ExStreak Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

ExStreak does not collect, transmit or sell your personal data. There is no account, no server and no analytics. Everything you log stays on your phone.

In one sentence: ExStreak stores your training data in a database on your own device, never uploads it anywhere, and works entirely offline.

What ExStreak stores on your device

The app saves the following locally, in a SQLite database and in Android's app preferences. None of it leaves your phone.

DataWhy it exists
Workouts, sets, reps, weights, durations To show your history, statistics and personal records.
Daily activity and streak history To calculate your current and longest streak.
Goals and earned badges To track progress toward targets you set.
A display name, motto and chosen avatar Optional. Used only to personalise the app's own screens. The avatar is one of a fixed set of emoji — there is no photo upload.
Optional age, height and weight Optional. Entered during setup, used only to display your figures in your preferred units. You can skip this entirely.
App preferences Theme, units, rest times, reminder time, rep-counting mode.

What ExStreak does not do

  • It does not create an account or ask for an email address.
  • It does not upload your training data to any server.
  • It contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDK.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.
  • It does not share or sell data to third parties, because it has no way to send data anywhere.

Permissions and why they are requested

PermissionUsed for
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Showing the daily training reminder and the streak-expiry warning — only the ones you switch on. Declining simply means no reminders.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, USE_EXACT_ALARM Delivering the daily reminder at the exact time you chose.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Re-scheduling your reminders after the phone restarts.
WAKE_LOCK Keeping the screen on during an active workout so it does not sleep mid-set. Can be turned off in Settings.
Proximity sensor (optional hardware) Counting reps when you enable proximity counting and rest the phone on the floor. The sensor is read only while a workout screen is open, and only in that mode. Nothing is recorded except the rep count. Phones without the sensor use tap counting instead.
INTERNET (debug builds only) Included automatically by the Flutter toolchain for development tooling. The published release build does not request it, and the app makes no network requests.

Sharing and exporting — your choice

ExStreak includes an export feature that writes your training data to a CSV or JSON file and hands it to Android's share sheet. This only happens when you tap it, and you choose where the file goes. Once you share a file with another app, that app's own privacy policy applies to it.

The "share your progress" button on the Profile screen composes a short text summary (your streak, record and total reps) and passes it to the share sheet. Again, nothing is sent anywhere until you pick a destination.

Deleting your data

Because the data is local, you are always in control:

  • Settings → Your data → Delete training data removes every workout, streak, record, goal and badge, keeping your profile.
  • Settings → Your data → Reset the app removes everything and returns the app to first-run setup.
  • Uninstalling ExStreak deletes the database along with the app.

There is nothing for us to delete on our side, because we never receive anything.

Children

ExStreak is a general-audience fitness app and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Google Play

ExStreak is distributed through Google Play. Google collects its own data about installs and crashes under its own policies, independently of this app. See Google's Privacy Policy.

If you have opted in to sending crash reports to Google Play, a crash may include a stack trace of the app's code. These reports contain no training data or profile information.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of ExStreak collects or transmits anything, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described in the release notes.
