## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Consistency beats intensity.

ExStreak is a training app for people who want to actually keep going. No
subscription, no account, no server — every feature is available to everyone,
and everything works offline.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A PLAN THAT ADAPTS TO YOU
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Six progressive plans — push-ups, pull-ups, dips, squats, sit-ups and plank —
each with three levels and sixteen days per level. Every session is laid out in
advance, so there is nothing to configure before you train.

After each workout you say how it felt: too easy, just right, or too hard. Too
easy jumps you a level. Too hard repeats the day. The plan meets you where you
actually are instead of where a spreadsheet thinks you should be.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STREAKS THAT SURVIVE REAL LIFE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Your streak is calculated from your real training history, not a number that
quietly ticks up. It counts calendar days on your device, so a session finished
at 23:58 counts for that day.

Train fourteen days in a row and you earn a streak freeze, up to two in
reserve. Miss a day and a freeze is spent automatically to bridge the gap — one
busy Tuesday does not erase a month of work. A freeze protects progress; it
never invents it.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TWO WAYS TO COUNT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Tap the counter ring after each rep, or switch on proximity counting: put the
phone on the floor and every rep is registered as your nose covers the sensor.
No tapping mid-set. Phones without a proximity sensor simply use tapping.

Timed holds like the plank get a countdown ring that ends the set for you.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
34 EXERCISES, AND ROOM FOR YOURS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Push-ups (standard, incline, wide, diamond), pull-ups, chin-ups, dips, rows,
shoulder press, lateral raises, biceps and hammer curls, triceps extensions,
squats, lunges, Bulgarian split squats, calf raises, glute bridges, wall sits,
plank, side plank, sit-ups, crunches, leg raises, Russian twists, burpees,
mountain climbers, running, jump rope and more.

Each one carries a description, coaching cues, its muscle group, the equipment
needed and a sensible rest time. Four kinds of tracking are supported: reps,
reps with weight, timed holds, and distance with duration.

Create your own exercises too, with your own cues and rest times.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
YOUR OWN SESSIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Build a workout by hand: pick exercises, set sets and targets, reorder them,
then train. Or use Free Practice — one exercise, no targets, go until you
cannot. Perfect for testing a max.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PROGRESS YOU CAN ACTUALLY READ
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• Volume trends over a week, month, quarter or year
• Best-set progression per exercise — real strength, not just more sets
• Estimated one-rep max for weighted work
• Muscle-group balance, so you notice what you are skipping
• Personal records for reps, holds and weight
• A streak calendar showing trained days, freezes and rest days
• Full session history, every set, with the targets you were given

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
GOALS AND BADGES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Set goals that track themselves: 25 push-ups in one set, 10 000 reps all time,
a 30-day streak, four sessions a week. Progress is recomputed from your real
data, so a goal is never wrong.

22 badges across streaks, lifetime volume, max reps, plank holds and a couple
of specials for the early risers and the night owls.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
REMINDERS, NOT NAGGING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

One optional daily reminder at a time you choose, and an optional evening
warning when a live streak is about to expire. That is the maximum. No
marketing notifications, ever.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

ExStreak has no account and no server. Every workout, streak and record lives
in a database on your device. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is sold, and there
is no analytics SDK.

Export everything to CSV or JSON whenever you want. Delete your training data
or reset the app completely, from Settings.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Dark by default, light theme available, metric or imperial units.

Start with one set. That is the whole trick.
```
